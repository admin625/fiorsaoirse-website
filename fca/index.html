Questions

What if my studio doesn't fit the typical mold?
FCA Studio learns your specific voice. The more you use it and refine it, the more it sounds like you. Studios in our founding cohort look very different from each other — Pilates, barre, hybrid HIIT/strength, and more. The product adapts.
How is this different from just using ChatGPT?
ChatGPT forgets your brand every session. It doesn't know your colors, your photo style, or your studio's voice. It doesn't generate matching images. And it doesn't know when your audience is online. FCA Studio is purpose-built for studio social — every piece connects.
What can I do during the 5-day trial?
Five days, five posts. Full product access — your gallery, AI assist, AI generation. Plenty of room to set your brand foundation, generate a week of content, see what FCA Studio sounds like in your voice, and decide if it's right for you.
What happens after the trial?
If you continue, your founder pricing ($299/mo) locks in forever — as long as you're one of the first 100 studios. After slot 100, standard pricing applies ($599/mo).
Do I need to be technical?
No. If you can set up an Instagram account, you can use FCA Studio.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No annual contracts, no cancel fees. We earn your renewal every month.
