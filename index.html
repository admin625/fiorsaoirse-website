About Fiorsaoirse

"Fior Saoirse" (fee-ur see-ur-sha) — Irish Gaelic for "True Freedom"

Fiorsaoirse is a one-person software company. I'm Mac — I build AI agents that are purpose-built for specific industries, not generic tools that try to do everything for everyone.

The name means True Freedom in Irish Gaelic. That's the point: freedom from generic AI that doesn't understand your business, your customers, or your workflow. Every agent I build starts by learning the language and patterns of one specific vertical — fitness, food, real estate, hospitality — and works the way people in that industry actually work.

I'm not a team. I'm a builder with strong opinions about how AI should serve real businesses. If that resonates, I'd love to hear from you.
